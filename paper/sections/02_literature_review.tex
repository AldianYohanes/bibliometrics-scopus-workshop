Modern LLMs rest on the Transformer \parencite{vaswani2017}, on scaling that allows few-shot learning \parencite{brown2020}, and on open foundation models that can be run and adapted outside the infrastructure of their developers \parencite{touvron2023}. Prompting methods that elicit step-by-step reasoning \parencite{wei2022} form part of the basis for LLM agents.

Autonomous agents have a long history, but earlier work often trained agents with limited knowledge in isolated environments. Research on LLM-based agents grew once LLMs absorbed broad knowledge from the web. The main survey in this area proposes a unified framework for how such agents are constructed and reviews their applications \parencite{wang2024agents}.

A separate body of work concerns running LLMs on devices. On-device LLMs are attractive for privacy, latency, and bandwidth, but device capacity limits what they can do. Mobile edge intelligence, which sits between on-device and cloud AI, is one answer to this limit \parencite{qu2025}. Two further surveys cover the architectures, optimisation, security, and responsible development of LLM-based edge intelligence \parencite{friha2024} and the full lifecycle of edge LLMs, from model design and pre-deployment to runtime optimisation and applications \parencite{zheng2025}. Small language models are favoured for their low inference latency and cost, which suits resource-limited settings \parencite{wang2025slm}. In the browser, WebLLM provides local inference through WebGPU and WebAssembly. Its authors argue that smaller open models and stronger consumer devices have made on-device deployment practical, and that the browser is both universally accessible and a natural environment for agents \parencite{ruan2024webllm}.

In operations and supply chains, a capability-based framework examines how AI and generative AI affect thirteen decision areas, among them demand forecasting and inventory management \parencite{jackson2024}. A survey of projects in the United States and the United Kingdom found that most aim at operational gains, such as customer satisfaction, lower costs, and efficiency, while the main obstacles concern data, technology, and organisation \parencite{wamba2023}. A review of explainable and neurosymbolic AI in supply chain management concludes that limited explainability still hinders adoption \parencite{kosasih2023}. More recent studies test LLMs on spare-parts demand forecasting \parencite{nakhost2025}, on multi-agent consensus in the supply chain \parencite{jannelli2025}, and on knowledge graphs for supply chain visibility \parencite{almahri2025}.

Bibliometric mapping has already been applied to LLM topics. A bibliometric and discourse analysis covered more than 5,000 LLM publications from 2017 to early 2023 \parencite{fan2024}. A study of LLMs in transportation used Scopus data with Bibliometrix, VOSviewer, and Python \parencite{hassan2025}. A bibliometric review of agentic AI in small, medium, and micro enterprises analysed 214 papers from 2019 to 2025 drawn from Scopus, Google Scholar, and grey literature \parencite{olujimi2025}. A review of 84 application studies of LLMs in business and management, drawn from Scopus, Web of Science, and Google Scholar, combines co-word analysis with a thematic synthesis and links LLM keywords to operations, finance, marketing, and supply chain management \parencite{arslan2026}; it does not cover models that run on the device. A conference study maps themes and trends of LLM-driven intelligent agents without the device or inventory streams \parencite{li2025agents}. Among the studies found in OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, and arXiv, none maps agent, on-device, and inventory research together; Scopus and Web of Science were not searched.

The surveys and the supply chain studies each describe one stream, and the bibliometric studies are broad or limited to one domain. What is missing is a joint map that shows whether the streams share foundations and vocabulary or stay apart, and where evidence is lacking for small businesses that might run such systems locally.
