\textbf{Foundations.} Modern LLMs rest on the Transformer \parencite{vaswani2017}, on scaling that enables few-shot learning \parencite{brown2020}, and on open foundation models that can be run and adapted outside the original developers' infrastructure \parencite{touvron2023}. Prompting methods that elicit step-by-step reasoning \parencite{wei2022} are part of the basis on which LLM agents are built.

\textbf{LLM-based agents.} Autonomous agents have a long history, but earlier work often trained agents with limited knowledge in isolated environments. The surge of research on LLM-based autonomous agents followed the knowledge that LLMs acquire from the web, and the main survey reviews how such agents are constructed and where they are applied \parencite{wang2024agents}.

\textbf{LLMs on the device and in the browser.} Running LLMs on edge devices is attractive for privacy, latency, and bandwidth, but device capacity limits what the models can do; mobile edge intelligence, which sits between on-device and cloud AI, is one answer \parencite{qu2025}. Surveys cover the architectures, optimisation, security, and responsible development of LLM-based edge intelligence \parencite{friha2024} and the whole lifecycle of edge LLMs from model design and pre-deployment to runtime optimisation and applications \parencite{zheng2025}. Small language models are favoured for low inference latency and low cost and suit resource-limited settings \parencite{wang2025slm}. In the browser, WebLLM provides local inference through WebGPU and WebAssembly, on the argument that smaller open models and stronger consumer devices have made on-device deployment practical and that the browser is a universally accessible platform and a natural environment for agents \parencite{ruan2024webllm}.

\textbf{LLMs in operations and supply chains.} A capability-based framework examines how AI and generative AI affect thirteen decision areas in supply chain and operations management, among them demand forecasting and inventory management \parencite{jackson2024}. A survey of supply chain projects in the United States and the United Kingdom found that most projects aim at operational gains such as customer satisfaction, cost reduction, and efficiency, and that the main challenges concern data, technology, and organisation \parencite{wamba2023}. A review of explainable and neurosymbolic AI in supply chain management shows that explainability remains a barrier to adoption \parencite{kosasih2023}. Recent studies test LLMs on spare-parts demand forecasting \parencite{nakhost2025}, on multi-agent consensus in the supply chain \parencite{jannelli2025}, and on knowledge graphs for supply chain visibility \parencite{almahri2025}.

\textbf{Earlier bibliometric studies.} A bibliometric and discourse analysis of the LLM literature covered more than 5,000 publications from 2017 to early 2023 \parencite{fan2024}, and a bibliometric analysis of LLMs in transportation used Scopus data with Bibliometrix, VOSviewer, and Python \parencite{hassan2025}. A bibliometric review of agentic AI in small, medium, and micro enterprises analysed 214 papers published between 2019 and 2025 from Scopus, Google Scholar, and grey literature \parencite{olujimi2025}. \textbf{Need for the present study.} The edge-LLM surveys, the agent survey, and the supply chain studies each describe one stream, and the bibliometric studies are broad or domain-specific. A joint map is needed to show whether the streams cite common foundations, share vocabulary, or stay apart, and where evidence is missing for small businesses that might run such systems locally.
