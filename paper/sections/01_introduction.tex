Large language models (LLMs) build on the Transformer architecture \parencite{vaswani2017} and have shown that a single model can perform many language tasks from a few examples \parencite{brown2020}. The release of open models \parencite{touvron2023} has made it practical to run them outside large data centres. Models can now be deployed in the cloud, at the edge of the network, on a phone, or inside a web browser; WebLLM, for example, runs LLM inference entirely within the browser by using WebGPU \parencite{ruan2024webllm}.

The research on these models is spread over streams that rarely cite each other. One stream studies LLM-based autonomous agents \parencite{wang2024agents}. A second stream studies how to run language models on resource-constrained devices, in surveys of edge and mobile-edge LLMs \parencite{friha2024,zheng2025,qu2025} and of small language models \parencite{wang2025slm}. A third stream applies generative AI and LLMs to operations and supply chains, including inventory management and demand forecasting \parencite{jackson2024,wamba2023,nakhost2025}. Small businesses, which often have limited cloud budgets and sensitive data, sit where these streams could meet, but it is not clear whether the literature connects them.

Earlier reviews cover one stream at a time. The edge-LLM surveys concentrate on architectures, optimisation, and deployment techniques \parencite{friha2024,zheng2025,qu2025}, the agent survey on how agents are constructed and applied \parencite{wang2024agents}, and the supply chain studies on the capabilities and perceptions of generative AI in operations \parencite{jackson2024,wamba2023} and on explainable AI \parencite{kosasih2023}. Bibliometric work on LLMs exists, but it is broad, covering over 5,000 publications up to early 2023 \parencite{fan2024}, or it is tied to one domain, such as transportation \parencite{hassan2025}. A bibliometric review of agentic AI in small, medium, and micro enterprises reports a sharp rise since 2022 that is driven by LLMs and multi-agent systems \parencite{olujimi2025}, but it does not include the on-device stream. \todo{recheck in Scopus and Web of Science for earlier bibliometric studies that join the three streams; this search was done only in OpenAlex}

A bibliometric analysis is suitable for describing the structure of a fast-growing field and showing where its streams connect or stay apart \parencite{donthu2021,zupic2015}. This study maps the 2023--2026 journal literature across the three streams and answers three research questions.
